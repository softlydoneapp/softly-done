Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026 · Softly Done

Softly Done ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the Softly Done mobile application, how it is used, and how you can delete it at any time.

1. Local-First Usage (No Account Required)

You can use Softly Done without creating an account. By default, your tasks, habits, categories, notes, focus sessions, and preferences are stored locally on your device and are not sent to our servers.

2. Optional Cloud Sync Account

If you choose to create an account to enable cloud backup and multi-device sync, we collect and store:

  • Account information: Your email address and encrypted authentication credentials.
  • App content: The tasks, habits, categories, notes, focus sessions, and focus presets you create so they can sync securely across your devices.

Cloud authentication and database storage are powered by Supabase. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and protected by row-level security so only your authenticated account can access your records.

3. Subscriptions and Payments

Optional paid subscriptions are processed exclusively by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat to verify subscription status and unlock premium features. We do not collect, view, or store your credit card or banking details.

4. Notifications

If you enable reminders or focus timer alerts, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

5. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide core app functionality, cloud backup, and cross-device synchronization.
  • To manage your subscription status and restore purchases.
  • To respond to support or feedback emails you send us.

We do not sell your personal data, display third-party ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We keep your synced data only while your account remains active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time:

  • Inside the app: Open Settings → Cloud Backup / Account → Delete Account. Deletion takes effect immediately.
  • Outside the app: Follow the instructions on our Account Deletion page or email softlydoneapp@gmail.com.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (including the EEA/UK under GDPR and California under CCPA), you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, email us at softlydoneapp@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Softly Done is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at softlydoneapp@gmail.com.
