Delete Your Softly Done Account

How to permanently delete your account and cloud data.

Option 1: Delete Immediately Inside the App (Recommended)

  1. Open Softly Done on your device and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Open the Cloud Backup / Account section.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and all synced cloud records are permanently deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, send us a deletion request from the email address associated with your Softly Done account:

Request Account Deletion via Email

Email requests are verified and processed within 7 days.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Your authentication account and email address.
  • All synced tasks, habits, categories, notes, focus sessions, and focus presets stored in the cloud.

No personal cloud data is retained after deletion.

Note on local data and subscriptions: Deleting your cloud account does not automatically cancel an active Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Please manage or cancel subscriptions in your store account settings. To remove data stored locally on your device, uninstall the app.
